Refund & Return Policy

We want our customers to be satisfied with their purchases. Please contact us as soon as possible if there is a problem with your order.

Standard Products

Return requests for eligible, unused products should be made within 14 days of delivery. Products should be returned in their original condition and packaging. Certain opened, used, damaged, or personalized products may not be eligible for return.

Custom PC Builds & Custom Orders

Custom-built computers and specially ordered or personalized components are made or purchased for an individual customer's order. Deposits may become non-refundable once parts have been ordered or work on the custom build has begun, as disclosed and agreed to with the customer before purchase.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us promptly with your order information and photos when applicable so we can review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

Approved Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time after a refund is issued may depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.
